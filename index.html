How it works

Set up in about a minute

Pro uses Android's accessibility permission for one thing only: putting a small window over your phone's clock on the lock screen. That window shows the part of your Atmo wallpaper that sits underneath, with its clock. It doesn't read your screen, see your notifications or what you type, or tap anything for you. Your photos never leave your phone.

  1. Open Pro Tools

    Open Atmo Engine Pro and tap the sparkle button next to settings.

  2. Turn on the permission

    Pro Tools walks you through it, including Android's "allow restricted settings" step.

  3. Cover your phone's clock

    Drag the cover over it. The clock's style, colour and position are set in Fine tuning, the same as always.

Pro isn't on Google Play, because Play only allows this permission for accessibility tools. You install it straight from this site.

What you need

Requirements

  • Android 13 or newer. The instant hide on unlock needs Android 14 or newer; on Android 13 the cover leaves a moment later.
  • An Atmo wallpaper on your lock screen, with its clock turned on.
  • The accessibility permission for Atmo Engine Pro. Since Pro comes from outside Google Play, Android first asks you to allow restricted settings for it. The app shows you how.

Every phone maker does the lock screen a little differently, and on a few phones nothing can be drawn over it. If Pro doesn't work on your phone, you get your money back. See the refund policy.

A heads-up about banking apps: a few banking and payment apps refuse to open, or show a warning, while any accessibility service is turned on. That's their own security rule, and it applies to every app that uses accessibility, not just Pro. See the FAQ below for what to do.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and it's yours.

Can I use it on more than one phone?

Yes, on up to 3 of your own phones at the same time.

I got a new phone. How do I move Pro?

Enter your key on the new phone. If all 3 places are taken, the app lists your phones and you can remove an old one right there. Reinstalling on the same phone doesn't use up a place.

Does it need the internet?

Only now and then. You need a connection to activate. After that, Pro checks your licence and looks for updates about once a day when you're online, and keeps working offline for up to 30 days.

Do I still need the free Atmo Engine?

No. Pro has everything the free app has, so you only need Pro.

Will my banking apps still work?

Most will. A few banking and payment apps won't open, or keep warning you, while any accessibility service is on, because they can't tell which ones are harmless. If one of yours does that, turn off the Atmo Engine Pro lock screen clock in your phone's accessibility settings while you use it, then turn it back on. Your wallpaper keeps working the whole time; only the lock screen clock takes a break.

How do I get updates?

Pro checks for new versions on its own. When one is out, it lets you know with a notification and an Update available message with what's new. Tap Update and Pro downloads and installs it for you; Android asks you to confirm, and the very first time to allow Pro to install apps. Your wallpaper, settings and licence stay as they are. You can also check yourself in Pro Tools, or download the latest version here any time.

Can I try new features early?

Yes. Turn on Beta updates in Pro Tools and you'll get new versions before they're marked stable. They're usually fine, but they may have the odd rough edge. Turn it off whenever you like.
